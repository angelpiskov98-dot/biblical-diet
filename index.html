Questions

Before you begin

Is this a weight-loss or medical diet?

No. It is a spiritual, historical and educational guide. It does not replace individualized advice from a doctor or registered dietitian.

Do I need to follow Old Testament food laws?

No. The guide presents them in historical and theological context, including New Testament teaching, so readers can apply principles according to conscience and conviction.

Is this only for the Daniel Fast?

No. Daniel Fast readers will find relevant material, but the guide covers a wider range of biblical foods, fasting practices, table traditions and principles for faithful living.

Can I read it on my phone?

Yes. Both PDFs can be opened on a phone, tablet or computer and printed for personal use.
